Your dental visit questions answered

Find helpful info for your appointment

How can I schedule a visit?

Book online, call us, or use our form. Select your date and time, and we’ll confirm your appointment.

Can I change my appointment?

Contact us at least 24 hours ahead by phone or email to reschedule. We’ll help you find a new slot.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept most major dental insurance plans. Share your details when booking so we can verify coverage.

Are there cancellation charges?

No fee if you cancel 24 hours in advance. Late cancellations may have a small charge.
